About Us

The dynamic duo of graffiti artists and street art enthusiasts, working toward the prime goal to keep the street art spirit alive and celebrating the beauty of imperfection and self expression.

Meet the Experts

Muznna Majid

I grew up surrounded by walls that spoke louder than words, and graffiti became my language. This brand is my way of bringing that street energy to life so I can help others express themselves as well and bring the street art to life. My products reflect me, my ideas and my feelings, and I want everyone to be given a chance to display their feelings through art.

Tallal Khurram

I created this brand to celebrate graffiti as more than just art... It's a lifestyle. Each one of my designs allowed me to represent an intricate part of my personality in a way I had no idea was possible. I want to share my designs with other people and give them a platform to share theirs.
